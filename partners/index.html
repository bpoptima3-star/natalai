70% of the sale price: $3.49 on a $4.99 reading, $4.19 on $5.99, $4.89 on $6.99. Every sale shows in your dashboard and you get an email the moment one lands.

How long does a click count?

30 days, last click wins. If someone clicks your link today and buys a reading three weeks later on the same device, it’s yours.

When do I get paid?

Monthly via PayPal once your available balance reaches $10. Each commission clears after the 7-day refund window.

Do I need a big following?

No. Small, engaged astrology accounts often convert best. Stories, Reels, bio links, Pinterest pins, blog posts and newsletters all work.
